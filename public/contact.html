Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 3-5 business days.

Can I return a product?

Yes. You can return items within 7 days of delivery.

How do I use a coupon?

Enter your code in the cart (for example WELCOME10) and click Apply before paying.

Where can I see my orders and invoices?

Log in and open My Orders. Each order has a printable invoice.

How do I write a review?

Open any product page, scroll to Ratings & Reviews and click Write a review.
